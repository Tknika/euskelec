A cookie is a small text file stored in your browser when you visit a website. It enables the Internet to remember your visit when you return to the same website. Cookies usually store technical information, personal preferences, content personalisation, usage statistics, links to social networks, access to user accounts, etc. The cookie adapts the web content to the user’s profile and needs. Without cookies, the services provided by any website would be significantly impaired.

## Cookies used on this website

In accordance with Spanish Data Protection Agency directives, we will list the cookies used by this website to provide you with the most accurate information possible.

This website uses its own cookies:

– Session cookies, to ensure that users writing comments on the blog are human and not automated applications. This is how we combat spam.

This website uses the following third-party cookies:

– Google Analytics: Stores cookies to produce statistics on website visit volumes and traffic. By using this website, you consent to Google handling information about you. You must therefore contact Google directly to exercise any rights in this regard.

– Social networks: Each social network uses its own cookies so that you can click on buttons such as 'Like' and 'Share'.

## Deactivating or removing cookies

You may exercise your right to deactivate or remove cookies from this website at any time via the notice on the homepage of [https://euskelec.eus](https://euskelec.eus/). These actions are carried out in different ways depending on your browser. Web browsers are where cookies are stored. You must therefore use your browse settings to exercise your right to remove or deactivate cookies. Neither this website nor its legal representatives can ensure that cookies are handled appropriately by browsers. For any doubts or queries on this cookies policy, please get in touch with TKNIKA via the contact section.
