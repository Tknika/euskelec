Euskelec is a competition in which students from vocational training centres in the Basque Country design and build **a 100% electric vehicle** using the same driving base: motor, motor controller and batteries.

The goal is to encourage students to work as a team and to establish experimentation and creation as a teaching strategy for imparting technical knowledge of electric vehicles.
