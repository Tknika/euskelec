Tknika, Centre for Applied Research and Innovation in Vocational Training in the Basque Country, is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with Spanish [Royal Decree 1112/2018](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2018-12699) of 7 September on the accessibility of public sector websites and mobile applications.

This accessibility statement applies to the website **euskelec.eus**.

## Compliance status

This website is **partially compliant** with Royal Decree 1112/2018 due to the exemptions and the non-compliances listed below.

## Non-accessible content

The content listed below is non-accessible for the following reasons:

### Non-compliance with Royal Decree 1112/2018

- The photos in the 2018 and 2019 albums have a generic text alternative ("Photo 1", "Photo 2"…) that does not describe what they show (requirement 9.1.1.1 of standard EN 301 549 v3.2.1, WCAG 2.1 success criterion 1.1.1).
- Some videos on the Training and Media pages, hosted on YouTube, may lack reviewed captions and audio description (requirements 9.1.2.2 and 9.1.2.5; criteria 1.2.2 and 1.2.5).
- The PDF documents of the rules and the challenges in Basque and English declare Spanish as the document language, so screen readers may read them with the wrong pronunciation (requirement 10.3.1; criterion 3.1.1).
- The poster of the 9th edition, in PDF, has no structure tags or text alternative (requirements 10.1.1.1 and 10.1.3.1; criteria 1.1.1 and 1.3.1).

### Disproportionate burden

Not applicable.

### The content is not within the scope of the applicable legislation

- Pre-recorded videos published before 20 September 2020 (article 3.4.b of Royal Decree 1112/2018).
- The OpenStreetMap map on the Contact page (article 3.4.d). The essential information, the address, is also given as text.
- The YouTube player, which is third-party content neither developed nor controlled by Tknika (article 3.4.e).

## Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was prepared on **24 November 2026**.

The method used to prepare it was a **self-assessment** carried out by the organisation itself: an automated check of every page of the site and a manual check of keyboard use, focus, colour contrast, reflow on 320-pixel screens, motion (the home video and the 3D car, which can be paused and stop when the system asks for reduced motion) and the downloadable documents, against standard EN 301 549 v3.2.1 (WCAG 2.1, level AA).

The statement was last reviewed on 24 November 2026.

## Feedback and contact information

You can send communications about accessibility requirements (article 10.2.a of Royal Decree 1112/2018), for example to:

- report any possible non-compliance of this website;
- tell us about other difficulties in accessing the content;
- send any other question or suggestion for improving the accessibility of the website;

by writing to [admin@euskelec.eus](mailto:admin@euskelec.eus) or by post to Tknika, Zamalbide auzoa z/g, 20100 Errenteria (Gipuzkoa), Spain.

By the same means you can submit:

- a **complaint** about compliance with the requirements of Royal Decree 1112/2018;
- a **request for accessible information** about content excluded from the scope of Royal Decree 1112/2018 under its article 3.4.

In a request for accessible information, state clearly the facts, reasons and request that show it is a reasonable and legitimate request.

Communications are received and handled by Tknika. Complaints and requests for accessible information will be answered within **twenty working days** at most.

## Enforcement procedure

If a request for accessible information or a complaint has been rejected, you disagree with the decision, or the answer does not meet the established requirements, you may file a **claim** under article 13 of that Royal Decree. You may also do so if you have received no answer within twenty working days.

The claim can be filed with Tknika by the same means given in the previous section.

## Optional content

The site is built with HTML5, CSS and JavaScript, and uses WAI-ARIA where needed. It uses no cookies or other tracking systems.
